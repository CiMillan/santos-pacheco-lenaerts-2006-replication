Expected output:
beta 0, any gap: 0.5
beta 1, equal payoffs: 0.5
beta 1, neighbour +1: 0.731
beta 1, neighbour -1: 0.269
beta 10, neighbour +1: 0.99995
beta 10, gap -500 (no overflow): 0.0
copy rate over 100000 draws, beta 1, gap +1: 0.73028

Why: beta = 0 is a coin flip whatever the payoffs (random drift); equal
payoffs are always a coin flip. At beta 1 a neighbour one point ahead is
copied 73% of the time, and one point behind still 27% -- unlike the
Santos rule, which never copies a worse-off neighbour. At beta 10 copying
a fitter neighbour is almost certain. The -500 line checks the overflow-
safe branch (hubs can be hundreds of points apart). The last line checks
next_strategy_fermi draws at that rate: 0.730 vs 0.731, within sampling
noise. A bug would show as anything other than 0.5 at beta 0, or an
OverflowError on the -500 line.
