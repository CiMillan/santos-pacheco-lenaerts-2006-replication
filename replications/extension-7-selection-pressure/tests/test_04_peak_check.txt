Why: same all-human sweep with 50 runs instead of 10. The peak is again
at beta 0.56 (0.985, 48 of 50 runs all-C), then a slow decline to 0.945
at beta 10 (43 of 50). That is the shape Pinheiro et al. (2012, Fig. 4)
report on BA networks: a value of beta that maximizes cooperation. But
the dip is small: 48 vs 43 of 50 runs, so with 50 runs the peak is a
tendency, not a sharp result. The runs_all_c column shows most runs end
fully cooperative or fully defecting, so the mean is mostly the share of
runs won by cooperators. A bug would show as the beta >= 0.56 rows
differing from test_03 by much more than run-to-run noise.
