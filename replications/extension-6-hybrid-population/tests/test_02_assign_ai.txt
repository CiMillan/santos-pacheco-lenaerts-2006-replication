Expected output:
degrees of 3 hubs-placed AI: [6, 9, 12]
top 5 degrees overall: [12, 9, 6, 5, 4]
random AI count: 10
top_hubs of 20 nodes: 1
fraction_c: 0.75 None

Why: on a 20-node Barabasi-Albert network, 15% AI = 3 nodes. With
placement "hubs" they must be the three highest-degree nodes (12, 9, 6),
which is exactly the top of the degree list. With "random", 50% of 20 = 10
AI nodes. top_hubs keeps the top 5% by degree, which on 20 nodes is 1 node.
fraction_c counts 3 cooperators out of 4 = 0.75, and returns None for an
empty group (e.g. the "ai" group when ai_fraction is 0), so an empty group
is never mistaken for 0% cooperation.
A bug would show as hub-placed AI with degrees lower than the top three.
