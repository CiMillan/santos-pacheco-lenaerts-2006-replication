Expected output (roughly -- seed makes it exact for this codebase, but treat
the pattern, not the decimals, as the thing to check):
T=2, S=-1 -> final cooperation = 0.00
T=1.2, S=-0.1 -> final cooperation = 0.68
T=1.05, S=-0.02 -> final cooperation = 0.95

Why: T=2, S=-1 is the harshest corner of the dilemma space (paper's Fig. 3),
where the paper itself says a strongly negative S is the main threat to
cooperation -- so full collapse there isn't a bug. As the dilemma gets
milder (T closer to 1, S closer to 0), cooperation survives better. This
three-point sweep exists to confirm the code responds to T/S the way the
paper says it should, not just collapses regardless of input.
