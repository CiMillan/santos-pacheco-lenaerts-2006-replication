Why: all human, below beta 0.1 payoffs barely matter, so runs drift to
all-C or all-D by chance and 10 runs give noisy means (0.24-0.51). From
beta 0.18 cooperation rises; it is highest at beta 0.56 (0.97), then
stays near 0.93-0.96 up to beta 10. Means like 0.800 are 8 of 10 runs
ending all-C. Whether 0.56 is a real peak is checked in test_04.
With 10% AI on the hubs no beta rescues cooperation, and it falls
steadily as beta grows (0.30 -> 0.06): the more humans follow payoffs,
the more they copy the high-earning, mostly defecting AI hubs. The
hubs column stays near 0.30 because the top hubs are the AI agents
themselves. A bug would show as the 10% rows rising with beta, or the
0% rows staying near 0.5 at large beta.
