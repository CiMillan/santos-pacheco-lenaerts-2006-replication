Why: motion_stats() reruns the swarm loop but also records what
run_swarm() throws away: velocities, and each agent's two targets.
  speed      = average |velocity| in the last 60 generations. ~0 means
               agents have stopped moving.
  stable C   = fraction of agents that are C in ALL of the last 60
               generations (real cooperation, not flicker).
  target gap = average distance between an agent's personal best and its
               neighbourhood best at the end.
What to check: speed is ~0 ONLY at c1=0, and clearly above 0 for every
c1 > 0. If speed were also ~0 at c1=0.25, the hypothesis in
CONCLUSIONS.txt would be wrong. The target gap is NOT ~0 at c1=0 -- that's
expected, because personal best is still recorded at c1=0 but has no
effect (it's multiplied by c1=0).
