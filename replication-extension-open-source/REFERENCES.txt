References -- open-source game theory extension
===============================================

MAIN PAPER
  Critch, A., Dennis, M. & Russell, S. (2022). Cooperative and
  uncooperative institution designs: Surprises and problems in
  open-source game theory. arXiv:2208.07006.
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.07006
    PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.07006
  What we use from it:
    - CUPOD(k) ("Cooperate Unless Proof Of Defection") and DUPOC(k)
      ("Defect Unless Proof Of Cooperation"), Section 2.
    - Prop. 3.1: CUPOD never exploits. Prop. 3.2: DUPOC is never exploited.
    - Theorem 3.4: CUPOD(k) vs CUPOD(k) -> (D,D) for large k.
      Theorem 3.7: DUPOC(k) vs DUPOC(k) -> (C,C) for large k.
      Both via the parametric bounded Loeb theorem (PBLT).
    - Section 3.5: DUPOCs reward legible cooperation.
    - Open Problem 2: when does a population containing DUPOCs evolve so
      that everyone rewards legible cooperation?
    - Open Problem 3: DUPOC(k) vs CUPOD(k), conjectured (D,C), unsolved.
    - Open Problem 9: does a bounded PrudentBot exist? If so, study
      population dynamics among CooperateBots, DefectBots, DUPOCs and
      PrudentBots, with proof-search cost in the payoffs.
