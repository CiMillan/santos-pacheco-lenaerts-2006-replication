Expected output:
always_defect: {'D'}
always_cooperate: {'C'}
coin_flip share C: 0.5023

Why: the three control players never look at payoffs, so their output
depends only on the rule. The two "always" rules must return one action,
every time (a set with a single letter). coin_flip must be close to 0.5
over 10,000 draws; 0.5023 is normal sampling noise (about 0.005 either way).
A bug would show as both letters in an "always" set, or coin_flip far
from 0.5, e.g. 0.0 or 1.0 (the comparison sign flipped).
