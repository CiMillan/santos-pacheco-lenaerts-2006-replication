Extension 8 -- extension 6 on the real FM network: design

QUESTION
  Does the hub collapse of extension 6 survive on a real agents network,
  the foundation-model supply chain, where the hubs ARE models?

WHAT CHANGES (vs extension 6)
  Only the network. Barabasi-Albert (m = 2, N = 500, a new graph per run)
  -> the giant component of the Ecosystem Graphs release fm-2024-05-d3c06a4,
  as packaged in fm-networks-labs (data not copied here):
  503 of 742 assets, 650 links. One fixed graph for every run; the seed
  changes starting strategies, random AI placement and every draw.

WHAT STAYS
  Game (R=1, S=-0.1, T=1.2, P=0), humans = Santos imitation rule, AI =
  stateless Q-learning (alpha = tau = 0.1, reward = payoff / degree),
  controls (always C, always D, coin flip), 10 runs, 300 + 60 generations,
  synchronous updates, mean of the last 60 generations. main_fm.py
  reproduces extension 6's run_hybrid exactly on extension 6's own graph
  (tests/test_02).

DECISIONS (all design choices, no source, unless stated)
  Links are undirected. The release lists upstream -> downstream
    dependencies; in the game both ends play each other and see each
    other's payoff. A directed game (only downstream copies upstream) is
    the obvious alternative and not run yet.
  Only the giant component (503 of 742): the other 239 assets sit in small
    pieces where cooperation would evolve on its own. The deck already
    shows this component.
  Every asset is a player, whatever its type (model, dataset, application,
    external). Who "is" human or AI is the open question for the
    supervisor (deck, question 4).
  Three AI placements: "random" and "hubs" as in extension 6, plus
    "models": every asset whose type is model plays AI (208 of 503 = 41%).
    It is the most literal reading of "the hubs are AI models"; 25 of the
    top 50 hubs are models.
  Same 10 runs and 360 generations as extension 6, for a side-by-side
    comparison. The all-human run is still rising slowly at 360 (0.54 ->
    0.61 by 1500); the AI placements are stable (checked to 1500).
  Null model: the same degrees, links rewired at random (rewire_keep_degrees,
    the paper's own edge-swap method for its scale_free_random network).
    It splits off small pieces (435 of 503 stay connected); kept as is.
  The data are read from the fm-networks-labs release and not copied into
    this public repo. Set FM_DATA to point elsewhere.
