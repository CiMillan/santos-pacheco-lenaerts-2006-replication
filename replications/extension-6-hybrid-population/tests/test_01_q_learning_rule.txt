Expected output:
after one D reward of 1.2: {'C': 0.0, 'D': 0.12}
share of C when Q_C = Q_D: 0.5023
share of C when Q_D is 0.2 higher, tau 0.1: 0.12

Why: update_q moves only the played action, D, a tenth of the way (alpha
0.1) from 0 toward the reward 1.2, so Q_D = 0.12 and Q_C stays at 0.
softmax_action with equal values should pick C about half the time. When
D is worth 0.2 more and tau = 0.1, the formula gives
p(C) = 1 / (1 + e^2) = 0.119, so about 12% C. The exact decimals depend on
the seed; the thing to check is ~0.50 and ~0.12.
A bug would show as Q_C changing too, or the shares flipped (D favoured
when Q_C is higher), which would mean the sign in the exponent is wrong.
