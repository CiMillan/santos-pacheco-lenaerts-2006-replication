Expected (exact numbers will vary a little, the PATTERN is what to check):
S \ T    0.5    1.0    1.5    2.0

  0.0   1.00   0.83   0.33   0.05
  0.5   0.99   0.91   0.65   0.38

Why: this is a small (4x4), fast version of the paper's Fig. 2/3 heatmaps.
Reading the table: cooperation should be highest top-left (low T = little
temptation to defect, high S = mild or no penalty for being exploited) and
should fall toward 0 as you move right (T increasing) or down (S
decreasing, more negative). If cooperation were HIGHEST in the
bottom-right corner instead, that would mean something is backwards in
the payoff matrix or update rule -- this table's shape, not just "it ran,"
is the actual check.

This version uses n=200, 10 realizations, tail=10 -- small and fast (a
few seconds) so it's cheap to re-run while developing. The paper's real
figures use N=10,000 and a much finer T/S grid -- see the note about
scaling this up (not built yet) before treating numbers here as
publication-quality.
