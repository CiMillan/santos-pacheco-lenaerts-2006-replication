Why: this turns sweep()'s {(T,S): cooperation_fraction} dict into an
image -- S on the y-axis, T on the x-axis, color = fraction of
cooperators (dark blue = 0%, dark red = 100%), same color scale as the
paper's Fig. 2/3.

Open figures/plot_heatmap_demo.png and check the SHAPE, same as test_08_sweep.txt:
cooperation should be highest (dark red) top-left (low T, high S) and
fall toward 0 (dark blue) as you move right (T increasing) or down (S
decreasing). If the colors were flipped -- high cooperation bottom-right
instead -- that would mean something is backwards in payoff.py or
update_rule.py, not a plotting bug.

This uses sweep.py's small demo grid (n=200, 4x4, 10 realizations) --
fast to re-run while developing. Not the paper's real resolution/scale
yet (see ARCHITECTURE.txt).
