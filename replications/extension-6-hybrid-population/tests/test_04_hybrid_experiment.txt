Why: the 0% rows must match extension 1's imitation result (0.899), since
with no AI the model is the paper's. They do, so both placements start from
the same baseline.
Random placement: cooperation falls steadily as AI agents are added
(0.90 -> 0.67 -> 0.36 -> 0.17).
Hub placement: only 10% AI on the hubs already drops cooperation to 0.08.
The "hubs" column (cooperation among the top 5% by degree) shows why: the
hubs no longer hold cooperators (0.94 -> 0.30).
Humans end up cooperating LESS than the AI agents (e.g. 0.06 vs 0.29).
That isn't a bug: AI agents keep exploring (softmax, tau 0.1, still picks C
about 1 time in 4), while humans copy the highest-payoff neighbour,
which is usually a defecting AI hub. See CONCLUSIONS.txt for the check.
At 100% AI both placements are the same population, so they must agree
(0.229 vs 0.227, differing only by random draws).
A bug would show as the 0% rows differing from 0.899, or the two 100% rows
disagreeing by much more than noise.
