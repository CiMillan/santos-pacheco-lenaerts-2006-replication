Extension 8 -- conclusions (tests/test_04, plus the 1500-generation checks)

1. The real network holds less cooperation, even with no AI.
   All human: 0.54 at 360 generations (0.61 by 1500) vs 0.90 on the toy
   network. Runs agree (0.55-0.67 at 1500), unlike the toy network's
   all-or-nothing runs. The rewired null with the same degrees gives 0.62
   (0.65 by 1500): the degree sequence -- sparse (average degree 2.6 vs 4),
   230 leaves, top hub 19 links vs ~58 -- explains most of the drop, not
   the exact wiring.

2. The hub collapse survives.
   10% AI on the hubs: 0.13 (toy 0.08); 0.10 by 1500. Random placement:
   0.43 (toy 0.67). Hubs again matter far more than random nodes.
   Relative to each network's baseline: FM -76%, toy -91%.

3. Still "hubs that never imitate", not Q-learning.
   10% hubs: coin flip 0.09, Q-learning 0.13, always D 0.04 (toy 0.08,
   0.08, 0.01). Always-cooperate hubs lift it to 0.74, above the
   all-human 0.54, but less than on the toy network (0.99): FM hubs are
   smaller, so a lever with less reach.

4. The literal scenario: every model asset plays AI (41%).
   Cooperation 0.22, humans 0.17. Between 25% at random (0.31) and 25% on
   the hubs (0.12).

5. Reading (my framing, no source).
   Moving to the real network changes the numbers but not the story: AI
   hubs that never imitate decide cooperation. The real network starts
   from a weaker position, so it has less to lose.
