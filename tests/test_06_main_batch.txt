Expected: runs 100 independent realizations (100 fresh networks, seeds
1..100) of a scale-free network, T=1.2, S=-0.1, 200 generations each, then
averages the cooperation fraction across all 100 at every generation.
Final averaged cooperation should be close to 0.67, and the last 10
generations of the averaged trace should be roughly flat (not still
trending up or down) -- that flatness is the sign it has converged, not
still noisy.

Takes about 30 seconds -- that's 100 realizations x 200 generations x
1000 agents, so it's doing real work, not hanging.

Why we need this (not just one run()): a single network + a single random
start is noisy -- rerun it and you'd get a somewhat different number. The
paper reports cooperation averaged over many independent realizations, so
run_batch() + average_trace() reproduce that averaging instead of reporting
one lucky/unlucky run.
