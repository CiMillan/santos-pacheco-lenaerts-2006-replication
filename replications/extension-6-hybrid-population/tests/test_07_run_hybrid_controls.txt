Why: 10% stubborn AI agents on the hubs, one run, 100 generations.
The "AI C" columns check the rule is wired in: from generation 1 on, the
AI group plays exactly its rule (0, 1, or about 0.5).
The last column is the finding: whatever the hubs do spreads to the
humans. Defecting hubs take cooperation to about 0.02, cooperating hubs
lift it to about 0.98, and even coin-flipping hubs pull it down to 0.10.
So the collapse does not need Q-learning: hubs that never imitate are
enough. The default ai_rule="q_learning" is unchanged: test_03 still gives
its expected output.
A bug would show as AI C values that don't match the rule, or all C near
0.9 for every rule (the AI agents are being ignored).
